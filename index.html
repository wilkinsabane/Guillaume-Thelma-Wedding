The Day I Met My Forever

The Story of Thelma & Guillaume

“We went to Teacher Training College to prepare for our careers. Little did we know that, somewhere between the lessons and the dreams, life was preparing one of its most beautiful lessons for us.”
Chapter I • Where It All Began

There are some people we meet in life, and there are some people life has been preparing us to meet. Sometimes, destiny doesn't announce itself with fireworks or loud promises. Sometimes, it walks quietly into our lives, wearing an ordinary face, on an ordinary day, in a place where we least expect to find the person who will one day mean the world to us.

For me, that place was our Teacher Training College.

I went there with dreams in my heart, books in my hands, and a future I was trying to build. Like every other student, I was thinking about my education, my career, and the kind of man I wanted to become. I had no idea that somewhere within those classroom walls, among the noise of students, the pressure of assignments, and the struggles of student life, I would meet a woman who would eventually become one of the most beautiful chapters of my life.

Her name was Thelma.

I cannot say that the moment I first saw her, the heavens opened or time stood still. But looking back today, I realize that some moments become special only after life reveals their meaning. What seemed like an ordinary encounter was, perhaps, the beginning of a story God was quietly writing for us.

At first, she was simply a girl I knew from college. Someone whose presence would gradually become familiar, whose smile would become memorable, and whose existence would slowly find a place in my thoughts. Neither of us could have imagined how far that simple connection would take us.

We were young, learning about life while preparing to teach others. We were surrounded by lessons, but little did we know that life itself was preparing us for one of its greatest lessons: love.

✦
Chapter II • Growing Through the Seasons

And love, as I would later discover, is not always about grand gestures or beautiful words. Sometimes, it begins with the smallest things—a conversation that lasts longer than expected, a smile that stays in your memory, a feeling you cannot quite explain, or the quiet happiness of seeing someone without having a particular reason to be happy.

Somewhere along the way, Thelma stopped being just another person in my world. She became someone whose presence mattered. Someone I wanted to understand. Someone whose happiness began to mean something to me. And without even realizing when it happened, my heart began making room for her.

Life did not promise us that everything would be easy. Growing up comes with its own struggles, responsibilities, disappointments, and unexpected turns. We had our own journeys to travel, our own dreams to pursue, and our own lessons to learn. But somehow, our story continued to hold a place for both of us.

Years passed, and life took us through different seasons. We grew, changed, learned, and experienced the realities that come with becoming adults. We discovered that love is not merely the excitement of the beginning; it is also the patience required in difficult moments, the forgiveness needed after misunderstandings, and the courage to keep believing in something beautiful even when the journey is not perfect.

And perhaps that is what makes our story so special to me.

We did not simply meet at college. We met at a time when neither of us knew the full story that life had in store for us.

Today, when I look at Thelma, I see more than the girl I met at Teacher Training College. I see a woman with whom I am preparing to build a home, share my dreams, raise a family, and face the uncertainties of tomorrow. I see someone whose name has travelled with me through different chapters of life and has now found its way into my plans for forever.

It touches me to think that the same place where we once worried about classes, examinations, and our future careers would become part of the foundation of our love story.

Back then, we were preparing to become teachers. We were learning how to guide others, how to be patient, and how to prepare for the future. Yet life was quietly teaching us something far more personal: that some encounters are worth remembering, some connections are worth nurturing, and some people become so deeply woven into our lives that imagining the future without them no longer feels the same.

✦
Chapter III • Choosing Forever

And now, here we are.

From college classmates to two people preparing to stand before our families and make a lifelong commitment. From the uncertainty of youth to the beautiful seriousness of choosing each other. From a meeting whose significance we could not fully understand to a wedding that will give our journey a new beginning.

As I prepare to marry Thelma, I find myself looking back with gratitude. Not because our story has been perfect, but because it is ours. It carries the memories of where we started, the lessons of the years behind us, and the hope of everything still waiting ahead.

If someone had told me back then that the girl I met at Teacher Training College would one day be the woman I would proudly call my wife, I might have smiled without understanding just how precious that future would become.

But today, I understand.

Sometimes, the greatest love stories do not begin with a promise of forever. They begin with two people simply crossing paths, unaware that one day they will look back and realize that the ordinary day they met was anything but ordinary.

Thelma, you were part of my story before I fully understood the story we were writing together.

And as we prepare to begin this next chapter, there is one thing I want you to know: among all the things life has taught me, one of the most beautiful is that meeting you was not just a moment in my past. It became a part of the future I now look forward to with all my heart.

We went to Teacher Training College to prepare for our careers. Little did we know that, somewhere between the lessons and the dreams, life was preparing one of its most beautiful lessons for us.

The lesson was love. The blessing was you. And the journey ahead is forever.

Forever yours, Thelma ❤️ Guillaume
